Our Story

Coffee as craft,
not commodity.

cafe began as a conversation between two friends who believed that truly great coffee deserved an equally considered space. We source single-origin beans from small farms, roast in small batches, and train every barista in the science and soul of extraction.

Our café is designed to feel like an extension of your living room — a place to think, connect, or simply exist in good company with an excellent cup.
